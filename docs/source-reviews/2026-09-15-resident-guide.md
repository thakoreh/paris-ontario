# Resident guide source review: 2026-09-15

The service finder links to the County's collection information and address-specific waste tool, not a guessed Paris-wide pickup day.[1]
Brant Transit describes itself as a pre-booked, shared-ride service; the guide sends readers to current booking rules rather than caching fares or times.[2]
Library membership information includes online-only memberships; identification requirements are left with the library's source page.[3]
The newcomer checklist links to ServiceOntario address-change guidance without inventing a single deadline for every document.[4]
Recreation guides cover swimming, fitness, camps, drop-in activities and community events; the site does not claim that places are currently available.[5]
Property-tax guidance links to payment options and tells readers to check their own bill.[6]
The County's report form covers municipal issues and distinguishes the County of Brant from the City of Brantford. Its CAPTCHA may prevent form submission; the guide includes the County's published general phone contact as an alternative.[7]
Health811 is explicitly for non-urgent advice and referrals and does not replace 911.[8]

## Editorial limits

These are manually reviewed service links, not live service-status feeds. The displayed review date is fixed to this review. Newcomer progress is browser-only; nothing is submitted to an external provider by ticking a step.
